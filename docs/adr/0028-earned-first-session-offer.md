# ADR-0028: Offer continuity after the first session and weekly choice

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-13

## Context

The first session proves that training fits her constraints. The weekly
choice is the strongest commitment moment, but notifications previously
interrupted it before Home turned into the paywall. The owner wants stronger
commercial conversion while preserving honest claims and user control.

## Decision

After the first qualifying session consumes the free allowance, finish and
any skill celebration lead to the weekly intention, then an optional trial
offer. Its copy repeats her real weekly choice. Purchase and restore close
the offer after a grant. Not now returns to Home and preserves her records.
The prompt still requires entitlement before generating another session.
Home itself remains accessible. Existing launch entitlement checks remain.

The offer skips the notification interstitial; reminders remain owed for a
later completed session. Participants assigned three free sessions do not
receive this first-session offer. No prices or free allowances change.

## Consequences

The commercial request follows experienced value and chosen commitment.
Paywall view and leave events identify the firstClose surface. Trial starts,
second-session completion, paid conversion and return rates must be measured
before claiming a conversion improvement. No fabricated reviews, analysis,
urgency or discount funnel is introduced.
