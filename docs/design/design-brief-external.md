# FITher — Application Design Brief

## Project

Design a premium mobile fitness application that creates adaptive
calisthenics workouts for busy professional women.

FITher should help users become stronger and feel more energized through
workouts that fit the time, energy, location, and physical condition they
have today.

Primary promise:

> Strength that fits your life.

Product promise:

> A workout that fits today.

Existing prototype:

https://fither-prototype.acikgozutku1.chatgpt.site

The prototype communicates the intended product flow. Improve it rather
than copying every screen literally.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Busy professional women
- Limited or unpredictable free time
- May train at home, in an office, or while traveling
- Wants strength, capability, energy, and improved mood
- May be a beginner or returning to exercise
- Values clarity, efficiency, quality, and intelligent personalization

Secondary audience:

- Frequent travelers

Future audience:

- Mothers with young children, after appropriate specialist and
  postpartum review

## Product positioning

FITher is not:

- A weight-loss app
- A calorie tracker
- A hardcore bodybuilding product
- A random workout generator
- An AI coach that invents exercises
- A social fitness feed

FITher is:

- Structured strength progression adapted to real life
- A credible, expert-designed training system
- Flexible without becoming random
- Encouraging without being sentimental
- Premium without becoming exclusive or intimidating

## Desired emotional response

Users should feel:

- Understood
- Capable
- Calm
- In control
- Motivated to begin
- Proud after completing even ten minutes

The product should never make users feel guilty, behind, judged, or
punished for having a demanding life.

## Design principles

### 1. One decision at a time

Use progressive disclosure. Do not place the entire daily check-in or
onboarding questionnaire on one screen.

### 2. Ten minutes is complete

Ten-minute sessions must look and feel like successful workouts—not
diminished versions of longer sessions.

### 3. Show the adaptation

Users should understand why today's workout fits them. Explain the most
important changes in plain language.

### 4. Confidence through restraint

Premium quality should come from typography, spacing, proportion,
precision, and interaction—not decoration.

### 5. Progress without punishment

Reward weekly momentum and mastery. Avoid fragile streaks, shame, or
loss-framed messaging.
